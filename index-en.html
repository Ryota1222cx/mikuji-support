Hitofuri Support

Hitofuri (ひとふりみくじ) is an iPhone and Android app that calculates your Kyusei-kigaku (a Japanese astrology based on your date of birth) and combines it with a daily omikuji (fortune slip) draw, so you can talk it over with an AI in Japanese.

Hitofuri's fortune-telling and AI replies are for entertainment. They do not replace medical, legal or financial judgement. Please consult the relevant professional or authority for health, legal or money matters.

FAQ

  • Drawing omikuji: Once a day. Opening it again the same day shows the same result.
  • AI consultations: A limited number per day are free. Once used up, the chat screen offers the paid plan. See the purchase screen for the exact count and price.
  • Writing about distress: If your message suggests self-harm or suicide, it is not sent to the AI; the app pauses the consultation and points you to public support services instead.
  • Morning notification: Scheduled on your device; the app sends no notification from a server. Allow notifications in OS settings to receive it.
  • Deleting your data: Open Settings in the app and tap "Delete data on this device". Deleting the app also removes it.
  • Cancelling a subscription: On iPhone, open Settings, your Apple Account, Subscriptions, and cancel Hitofuri. On Android, open the Google Play Store, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, and cancel Hitofuri. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Moving to a new phone: Your date of birth and other entries live on your device. Purchases can be brought back with "Restore purchases" in Settings.
